# ADR-0004: Intended public licensing split and reserved marks

- **Status:** Accepted (records Foundation licensing direction; legal files remain drafts)
- **Date:** 2026-08-30
- **Source:** `PROJECT_FOUNDATION.md` sections 15, 16 and 24

## Context

The project aims for broad open and commercial implementation while retaining
attribution, legal clarity and control over official identity and conformance
claims. Bootstrap development precedes deliberate public licensing.

## Decision

At public release, specification and explanatory documentation are intended
for CC BY 4.0; schemas, SDKs, tests, conformance tooling and reference code are
intended for Apache-2.0. GeesNO names, logos and official conformance or
certification marks remain reserved under a separate trademark policy. Draft
license files during S0 do not promise stable compatibility or create an
official certification program.

## Consequences

- Commercial implementations need not open their own applications merely for
  implementing the public standard.
- Public release requires complete official license texts, attribution and
  notices.
- Contributor sign-off/relicensing terms and a mature essential-patent policy
  remain open for governance and legal review.
- Compatibility statements cannot imply endorsement or certification.
