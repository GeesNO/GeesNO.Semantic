# Universal model responsibilities and cardinalities

**Scope:** Universal abstract-model responsibilities and cardinalities.
**Architectural authority:** [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) and accepted ADRs.

Cardinalities below describe the accepted universal abstract model. “Required” means a
semantic relationship exists for an occurrence of that primitive; it does not
imply a JSON field or nesting choice. `0..*` means the capability is available
while content may be absent during incremental enrichment.

| Primitive | Responsibility | Conceptual identity | Required relationships | Optional relationships / cardinalities | Invariants | Lifecycle implications | Extension points | Further Profile and interoperability boundaries |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SemanticDocument | Bound one semantic dataset and declare its governing spec context. | One stable document identity. | Specification version `1`; identity `1`. | Entities `0..*`; Sources `0..*`; vocabulary context `0..*`; revision context `0..1`; profiles `0..*`; extensions `0..*`. | Empty/metadata-only/fully redacted datasets remain possible; storage pattern does not alter meaning. Contained state is authoritative only for its declared scope and Revision. | ADR-0007 accepts standalone state plus optional, constrained Revision/Change history. | Document-level namespaced data. | Canonical JSON representation follows accepted identifier, Vocabulary, lifecycle and Profile contracts; cross-document packaging remains separate. |
| Entity (abstract) | Provide the common identifiable semantic subject. | One stable semantic identity. | Kind `1` (`Concept` or `Representation`). | Labels `0..*`; assertions `0..*`; bindings `0..*`; extensions `0..*`. | Identity is not position, label, locator, hash or native ID; incomplete semantics are valid. | Identity continues across revisions; deletion must not silently reassign it. | Entity-level namespaced data. | Localized-label and enrichment conventions may be Profile-specific; Assertion placement follows ADR-0011. |
| Concept | Denote a thing independently of a digital occurrence. | Inherits Entity identity. | Entity invariants only. | Representation associations `0..*`; direct Bindings `0..*`; all assertions `0..*`. | Neither Representation nor Binding is required. | Survives replacement/removal of individual Representations. | Entity extensions and vocabulary classification. | Concept equivalence criteria depend on applicable semantic policy; Concept-Representation association placement follows ADR-0011. |
| Representation | Denote a particular digital occurrence. | Inherits Entity identity, distinct from source identity. | Entity invariants only. | Concept associations `0..*`; Bindings `0..*`; representation assertions `0..*`. | May predate known Concept or complete Binding; associations are independent claims, not automatically true/equivalent. | May be replaced or disappear without deleting its Concept. | Representation-specific namespaced data/profiles. | Representation payload conventions depend on applicable Vocabularies/Profiles; association placement follows ADR-0011. |
| Source | Give a reference context in which Bindings can be interpreted. | One mandatory ADR-0009 identity in JSON under accepted ADR-0013. | Source identity `1`. | Descriptive metadata `0..*`; referenced by Bindings `0..*`; trust/revision descriptors `0..*`. | ID-only Source is valid; a Source does not auto-dereference and is not an Entity kind. | Source revision/fingerprint/location may evolve under the same continuing origin context; none derives identity. | Source-description extensions. | Source-descriptor Vocabularies/Profiles; Core JSON representation follows accepted ADR-0013. |
| Binding | Correlate one Entity with one Source context/native location. | One mandatory ADR-0009 ID denoting one specific correlation under accepted ADR-0013. | Bound Entity `1`; Source context `1`. | Locator `0..1` in JSON; trust/lifecycle metadata `0..*`; extensions `0..*`. | Optional for every Entity; locator is Source-scoped, descriptive, non-executable and neither identity nor truth; equal Entity/Source/locator does not derive identity. | Same ID may survive locator correction/relocation only for the same correlation/occurrence; a distinct occurrence or changed anchor requires a new ID. | Qualified Binding method and locator value. | Locator Vocabularies/Profiles; Core JSON representation follows accepted ADR-0013. |
| Type / Classification Assertion | Claim that an Entity has a qualified type. | Mandatory serialized ADR-0009 ID under accepted ADR-0011. | Subject Entity `1`; qualified type `1`. | Provenance `0..*`; Confidence `0..*`; Review `0..*`; lifecycle `0..*`; extensions `0..*`. | Multiple/conflicting classifications may coexist; type is not a core Entity kind. | A changed claim body gets a new ID; lifecycle may supersede it without replacing the Entity. | Assertion extensions; external vocabulary term. | Conflict-selection policy belongs to applicable Profiles; qualified terms and lifecycle follow accepted contracts. |
| Property Assertion | Claim a qualified property value or explicit semantic value condition. | Mandatory serialized ADR-0009 ID under accepted ADR-0011. | Subject Entity `1`; property term `1`; exactly one value or explicit condition `1`. | Datatype context as needed; trust/lifecycle `0..*`; extensions `0..*`. | Absence is not unknown; canonical quantity always materializes its unit. | A changed property/value/condition body gets a new ID; history may retain the old claim. | Qualified property and structured-value extensions. | Repeated-claim versus aggregate-value policy belongs to applicable Vocabularies/Profiles; lifecycle follows ADR-0014. |
| Relation | Connect two Entities through a qualified predicate. | Mandatory serialized ADR-0009 assertion ID under accepted ADR-0011. | Subject `1`; predicate `1`; object `1`. | Provenance, Confidence, Review, lifecycle and extensions each `0..*`. | Predicate semantics come from vocabularies; direction is explicit; one global placement; out-of-document target availability is not guaranteed and no dereference is automatic. | A changed subject/predicate/object gets a new ID; old relation may be retained/superseded. | Predicate vocabularies and relation extensions. | Cross-document availability is not guaranteed; n-ary conventions require an applicable Vocabulary/Profile; lifecycle follows ADR-0014. |
| Provenance | Explain the origin/method/evidence of a scoped record or claim. | Optional ID; accepted ADR-0014 requires it for independent targeting/lifecycle continuity. | Target scope `1` by nesting; origin mechanism `1`. | Source/evidence `0..*`; actor/process `0..1`; time `0..1`; method/tool version `0..*`. | Does not establish truth; pseudonymous process identity is sufficient; chains are possible. | Pure scoped metadata may remain anonymous; a late ID cannot prove continuity with an earlier anonymous occurrence. | Qualified methods/evidence types; Accepted ADR-0015 Extension host. | Origin/evidence Vocabularies and optional Profile-defined provenance-chain conventions. |
| Confidence | Express one scoped certainty assessment. | Optional ID; accepted ADR-0014 requires it for independent targeting/lifecycle continuity. | Target scope `1` by nesting; score `1`. | Method/calibration, assessor and time `0..1` each; extensions `0..*`. | Independent of provenance/review; missing is not certainty; scores are not comparable unless calibration says so. | Multiple assessments may coexist; pure scoped metadata may be anonymous but independently historical state needs identity from first serialization. | Calibration/profile metadata; Accepted ADR-0015 Extension host. | Aggregation and calibration Vocabularies/Profiles. |
| Review | Record one scoped human or controlled-process disposition. | Optional ID; accepted ADR-0014 requires it for independent targeting/lifecycle continuity. | Target scope `1` by nesting; disposition `1`. | Reviewer/process, time, note/evidence and correction reference `0..1` or `0..*` as later defined. | Independent of Confidence; actor need not be personal; nested review-of-review is possible. | A simple nested Review may remain anonymous; a cited, superseded or historically continuous Review needs identity from first serialization. | Review-policy/profile metadata; Accepted ADR-0015 Extension host. | Disposition Vocabularies, transition policies and authority conventions. |
| Revision / Change | Preserve and describe semantic evolution. | Accepted ADR-0014 gives every Revision a mandatory ID and Change a conditional ID for independent addressability. | Revision ID `1` when present; parents `0..*`; Change revision `1`, targets `1..*`, qualified kind `1`. | Optional Change details/trust; validity, tombstones, supersession and history `0..*`. | ADR-0007/0014 require state-first consumption, optional non-replay history, explicit tombstone, no ID reuse and scoped tri-state completeness. | Entity, Assertion, Binding, association and Source description evolve independently; multiple parents express lineage only. | Qualified scope, Change and validity kinds/profile policy. | Lifecycle Vocabularies/Profiles, retention and external packaging; Core JSON shape follows accepted ADR-0014 and ADR-0016. |
| Extensions | Carry non-core, namespaced inert data. | Namespace-qualified extension identity/term `1`; record identity normally unnecessary. | Host `1`; qualified namespace/term `1`; data `1`. | Profile requirement `0..*`; lifecycle metadata only if host model permits it. | Cannot redefine/disable core, execute code or invalidate core merely by being unknown. | Accepted ADR-0015 distinguishes reading, lossless preservation and explicit lossy projection. | This is the extension mechanism itself. | Vocabulary publication/discovery follow accepted ADR-0018 and ADR-0021; applicable Profile constraints remain separate from bare Core. |

## Availability versus mandatory presence

Only the minimum identity/discriminator/claim-body relationships are required
for an occurrence of a primitive. Provenance, Confidence, Review, Binding,
classification, properties, relations, revisions and extensions are universal
**capabilities**, not mandatory content in every Entity or document.

This distinction is essential for identified-but-unclassified data,
Representations without known Concepts, source descriptions that are partial,
redacted datasets and enrichment across revisions.
