# Trademarks (DRAFT)

The intended open licenses for specification and code do not grant trademark
rights. `GeesNO`, `GeesNO.Semantic`, associated logos and future official
conformance or certification marks remain reserved for a separate policy.

Until that policy and an official certification program exist, no party may
claim certification or endorsement. Accurate, non-misleading compatibility
statements should identify the exact pre-1.0 specification/profile version.

This draft does not define complete nominative-use rules, logo rules,
certification criteria or enforcement procedures.
