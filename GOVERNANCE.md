# Governance (DRAFT)

[ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) defines the public architectural baseline.
Normative specifications and accepted ADRs refine that baseline. Schemas,
conformance tooling and SDK behavior do not override accepted semantics.

## Decision process

- Accepted architectural decisions are recorded in ADRs and changed only through an explicit subsequent architectural decision.
- A new normative choice requires an ADR with context, decision, consequences
  and compatibility impact.
- Intentionally open questions remain open until that process is completed.
- The specification precedes schema, tests and non-normative implementations.
- Conformance criteria must be public, exact-versioned and deterministic.

Maintainer roles, voting/consensus rules, release authority, contributor terms,
appeals and a mature patent policy are not established by this draft. They must
be defined before governance depends on external organizational participation.
